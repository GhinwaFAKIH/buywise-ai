## Publication checklist
- Screen-record the actual BuyWise demo; don't imply a product was personally tested.
- Link directly to the matching guide instead of sending visitors to an unrelated homepage.
- Include accessible captions/text overlays.
- Disclose affiliate compensation where relevant.
- Avoid medical claims and unverifiable before/after outcomes.
- Measure Google Search Console clicks/impressions and analytics landing pages after seven days.
